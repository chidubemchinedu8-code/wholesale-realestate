Gross spread at MODERATE, contracting at $95,000: `$255,462 − $95,000 − $50,307 = $110,155`. That is
**gross spread, not profit** — holding, closing, resale and the buyer's own margin live inside the
30% the 70% rule reserves.

**The honest reading: the ask clears the MAO at LIGHT and MODERATE, and only just at HEAVY.** That
is different from the Cleveland file where most leads died. But a $1,955 margin at HEAVY is not a
margin — it is a coin flip on whether the HVAC is alive. Do not present the HEAVY case as a deal.

## What could make this wrong

1. **No error band exists for Central Hilltop.** The repo has a backtested band for Cuyahoga; it has
   none for 43204. The dispersion here is measured (10.4%) but the *bias* is unmeasured. Do not
   quote $255,462 as precise and do not import the Cuyahoga band — dispersion is local.
2. **The median-$/sf ARV has never been checked against an actual resale in this hood.** AUDIT.md
   stands. This is a screening number.
3. **131 S Eureka at $177/sf** means renovated product here trades in a real range, not a point. If
   the subject finishes at Eureka's level the ARV is higher; if it finishes like 204 S Wheatland it
   is lower. The median deliberately splits that.
4. **`$95,000` was never tested against the seller.** The MAO is a ceiling, not an offer. Opening
   below it requires a BATNA in writing first (`scripts/negotiate.py --check`).
5. **Electrical/HVAC scope unverified** — see above. This is the single biggest driver of grade.
