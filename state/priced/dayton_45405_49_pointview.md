### What this history says, and it is not bullish

This house has been on and off the market **continuously since June 2024** —
28 months — across two MLS numbers and repeated price cuts from $125,000 to
$59,900, a **52% decline**. It went pending twice before (2025-04-02 and
2026-08-12); the 2025 pending evidently died and relisted two months later.

A 52% cumulative cut with no takers, plus a dead pending, is the market's
verdict on condition, not on price. **The 2022 seller took $135,000 in a market
that was not weaker than today's.** Since then it has been marketed to renters
($1,250 and $1,300/mo) and then sold off.
