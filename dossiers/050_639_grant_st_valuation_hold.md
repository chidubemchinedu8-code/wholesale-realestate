The current MLSNOW syndication classifies the property as an active **single-family** 3/2, 1,853-sf Colonial at $115,000. A public property-record mirror instead identifies the same address as a **duplex (two units, any combination)**. That conflict changes the applicable comp set and valuation methodology.

- **Public-record conflict:** Duplex/two-unit classification at the same 3/2/1,853-sf address.
- **Condition/marketing:** Current marketing says investor/rental or fix-and-sell potential but does not identify unit configuration, separate entrances/meters, lease/rent roll, certificate of occupancy, or permitted use.

## Disposition

**No preliminary ARV calculated — completed legal-use valuation hold.** Do not apply renovated single-family comps to a legal duplex, or income/multifamily metrics to a single-family use, until the discrepancy is resolved. Obtain county parcel/assessor and zoning records, MLS sheet, floor plan, legal-unit/CO/POS/permit history, utility-meter configuration, current occupancy/lease/rent data, and title. Then value under the established legal use with appropriate closed sales (and an income approach where appropriate).

No rehab, MAO, or offer anchor is calculated.
