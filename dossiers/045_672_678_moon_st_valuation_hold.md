## Disposition

**No preliminary ARV calculated — completed material package/configuration/utility valuation hold.** A credible value cannot be assigned to the listed package or 678 alone until the seller provides:

1. Separate parcel/APN, legal-use, title, lot, tax, utility, and occupancy information for **both 672 and 678**;
2. Current MLS sheets and floor plans for each building, including current and proposed GLA, bedrooms/baths, third-floor stair/egress, garage treatment, and permits/inspection/POS/code status;
3. Utility status and documentation for water, sewer, electric, gas, HVAC/rough-in, and certificate-of-occupancy requirements;
4. Written tax-abatement program eligibility, term, approvals, and restrictions;
5. 3–5 renovated **closed** comparable sales for each legal property (or a documented package/income valuation if the intended legal use is multi-unit).

Do not treat future/potential bedrooms as current legal bedrooms, distribute this as a completed/turnkey deal, or calculate rehab, MAO, or an offer anchor from the incomplete package facts.
