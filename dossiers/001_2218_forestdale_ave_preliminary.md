### Basis

The 4-bedroom updated-sale evidence brackets the working subject in roughly the low-$110s to low-$120s per square foot after accounting for the subject’s single bath and smaller footprint. Applying a cautious roughly **$120–$133/sf** to the 1,086-sf MLS/intake size yields approximately **$130,000–$145,000**. The notably higher $158/sf Roanoke result is treated as an upside retail marker rather than the primary basis.
