# Repair estimation

A repair number is a **range plus a contingency**, unless an inspection report or a
contractor bid exists. Photos support a range, not a number.

## Categories

| Category | Meaning | Typical share of a heavy job |
|---|---|---|
| Light | Paint, flooring, minor kitchen/bath, cosmetic exterior | cosmetic only |
| Medium | Full kitchen and baths, flooring throughout, some mechanicals | cosmetic + 1–2 systems |
| Heavy | Multiple systems + cosmetic + exterior + partial structural | cosmetic + 3+ systems |
| Major / structural or unknown | Foundation, roof structure, sewer, fire/water damage, or unverifiable systems | define separately, never buried in a range |

## Line items

Kitchen · bathrooms · flooring · paint · drywall · windows · roof · HVAC · plumbing ·
electrical · exterior (siding, soffit, gutters, porch, garage) · structural concerns ·
contingency.

For each line, record:

```
Item | Observed issue or reason for inclusion | Low | High
Basis: visible / reported / assumed | Confidence: high / medium / low
```

**An item with no observed issue and no report does not get a cost line.** "Assume
$8,000 for HVAC" without evidence is how a repair number becomes fiction. If it is
genuinely unknown, it goes in the unknown-systems allowance and the report says why.

## Contingency

| Situation | Contingency on the itemised total |
|---|---|
| Photo-based, all systems visible/updated | 10% |
| Photo-based, one system unverifiable | 15% |
| Photo-based, two or more systems unverifiable | 20–25% |
| No interior photos at all | Do not produce an itemised total. One broad range, wide, labelled `UNKNOWN` |
| Inspection report or contractor bid | 5% (change orders, scope creep) |

Do not use the contingency to smuggle a number in, and do not shave it to make a deal
pass. If the deal only works at 5% contingency on a 1920 house with no interior photos,
the deal does not work.

## The unknown-systems allowance

Roof, HVAC, electrical service, plumbing, foundation, sewer. Where these cannot be
verified, price a single explicit allowance and state the effect on the offer:

```
Unknown systems allowance: $15,000 (roof age, panel capacity, sewer not verified)
Effect: seller MAO falls $15,000. If a walkthrough confirms the roof is 5 years old,
this allowance drops to $5,000 and the MAO rises.
```

That sentence is worth more than the estimate — it tells the operator which photograph
to ask for.

## Selling-price sanity check

Cross-check the total against the ZIP's typical rehab cost per sqft for the condition
class. Akron's reference points, from the completed screens: a heavy rehab on a 1,900
sqft 1919 house ran **$55,000–$80,000** (~$29–$42/sqft); a light refresh on a 1,500 sqft
house ran **$30,000–$40,000**. A total far outside the local band needs a stated reason.

## Never

- A precise number where the evidence is a range
- A low estimate to make the deal pass
- Repair costs with no observable or reported basis
- A single figure with no contingency on a photo-based estimate
- Presenting a photo-based estimate as equivalent to an inspection
