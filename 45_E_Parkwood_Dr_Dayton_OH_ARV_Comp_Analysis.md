### Comp-set readout

- **Distance:** 0.20–0.33 mile; every selected comp is well inside the 1-mile limit.
- **Recency:** all five sales are within the six-month priority window.
- **Price per square foot:** $93.23–$129.92; **median $112.29/sq ft** and **mean $110.46/sq ft**.
- **Functional comparison:** every selected comp is a larger 3-bedroom home (1,294–1,544 sq ft); three have 1.5 baths. The subject is a materially smaller 2/1, so raw sale prices of $140,500–$176,950 are **not** direct ARV indications for the subject.

## ARV conclusion — completed, retail-ready condition

A simple $/sq-ft cross-check produces:

- Mean comp $/sq ft × 976 sq ft = **$107,810**
- Median comp $/sq ft × 976 sq ft = **$109,590**

Those mechanical indications must be moderated. The subject has 318–568 fewer above-grade square feet than the comps and has one fewer bedroom than every comp; it also lacks the half bath present in three comps. A smaller house can carry a somewhat higher $/sq-ft figure, but that does not fully offset the bedroom/bath utility deficit. Conversely, the subject needs to be genuinely comparable in finish quality, systems, curb appeal, and sale-ready presentation before using renovated retail sales at all.

### Cautious supported ARV: **$100,000–$110,000**

**Working ARV for conservative underwriting: $105,000.**

- Use the **low end** if the project is a basic cosmetic refresh, if any major system/permit issue remains, or if the completed layout shows smaller/less-functional rooms than the comp set.
- The **$105,000–$110,000** portion is supportable only for a complete, clean retail renovation with a modern kitchen and bath, durable flooring/paint, sound mechanicals, and strong listing presentation.
- Do **not** use 44 E Beechwood’s $129.92/sq-ft result alone. Applying it directly would imply about $126,800 before recognizing that 44 E Beechwood is a 3/1.5 with a notably strong finish package.
- The conclusion is a broker/wholesaler market screen, **not an appraisal**. Verify MLS agent remarks, interior condition, permits, basement/finished-area treatment, mechanical ages, and current buyer demand before setting a resale list price.
