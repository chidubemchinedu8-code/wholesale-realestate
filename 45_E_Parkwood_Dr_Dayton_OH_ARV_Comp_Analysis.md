### Why the 2-bedroom sales should control

The subject is a 976-sq-ft 2/1. The three primary comparables are all 2/1s and only 41–78 sq ft larger. That is a materially better functional match than treating 1,294–1,544-sq-ft 3-bedroom properties as equal-weight evidence. The larger homes are useful for understanding the neighborhood's renovated retail ceiling and finish expectations, but their added bedroom, extra bath utility, and 318–568 additional square feet can otherwise overstate subject value.

## Revised ARV conclusion — completed, retail-ready condition

### Cautious supported ARV: **$98,000–$106,000**

**Working ARV for conservative underwriting: $102,000.**

- **$98,000–$102,000:** appropriate if the renovation is clean but basic, if the subject lacks the garage/basement utility found in some direct comps, or if material system, layout, or permit issues remain.
- **$102,000–$106,000:** requires a genuinely complete retail renovation comparable to 245 Victor's finish and functional condition—modern kitchen and bath, durable flooring/paint, sound mechanicals, and strong curb appeal/listing presentation.
- **Above roughly $106,000** needs stronger support than the current direct 2-bedroom sales provide. It would depend on matching or exceeding 50 Sandhurst's high-side condition and showing comparable features.
- The very close 3-bedroom comps establish that renovated buyer demand exists, but they are **not** a reason to carry their raw $/sq-ft or total prices straight into a smaller 2/1 valuation.

This is a broker/wholesaler market screen, **not an appraisal**. Before setting a resale list price, verify property condition, room count/layout, finished-area treatment, mechanical ages, permits, title, and current active/pending competition.
